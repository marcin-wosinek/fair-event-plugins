Explain when online ticket sales have closed. Once every sale period of an event has ended, the Event Signup block now shows a neutral notice, "Online ticket sales for this event have closed.", in place of "Ticket sales are temporarily unavailable. Please check back later.", both on the signup view and on the payment-retry view. The temporary-unavailability message stays for payment problems, sale periods that have not opened yet, gaps between periods and incomplete sale-period configuration. What can be purchased is unchanged.
