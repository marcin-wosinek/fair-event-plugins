Store activities and check-in per ticket. A purchaser with several tickets can have different activities on each and be checked in one ticket at a time from the Audience tab. Activities added later go to the chosen ticket. Earlier participant-level activities and check-ins are moved to a ticket only when the participant holds exactly one ticket for that date. Otherwise they stay visible as participant-level history.
