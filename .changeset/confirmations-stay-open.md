Confirmations for deleting a registration, deleting an event, changing an event's publication and deleting a Mail History record now stay open until the request succeeds. While it runs they cannot be dismissed or confirmed twice, and a failure is shown inside the dialog so you can retry or cancel. Their titles, and those for ending a series and deleting a form submission, name the registration, event, series, record or submission they act on.
