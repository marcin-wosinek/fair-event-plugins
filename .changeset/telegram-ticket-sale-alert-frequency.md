Telegram ticket sale alerts can now be sent as a daily or weekly digest for each organizer, instead of one message per sale. Fair Events → Event notifications sets each organizer's frequency, delivery time and, for a weekly digest, weekday in the site's timezone, and shows when the next digest is sent and how many sales are waiting for it. A digest lists each event with its tickets and totals, and nothing is sent for a day or week without sales. Organizers set up before this change keep receiving one message per sale. Changing the frequency never drops or repeats a sale: waiting sales move to the next digest, and switching back to one message per sale sends them in a final digest. Organizer emails and Telegram payment notifications are unchanged.
