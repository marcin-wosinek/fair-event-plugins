The individual weekly email now looks like the weekly email to subscribers. It has the site-name header, the events grouped by day with their time and a link, a note on events from other websites, a sign-off and a site-name footer, and it fits phone screens. Both emails get their events and content from Fair Events, so the same week lists the same events in both.

The weekly email to subscribers keeps its greeting and subscription link. Its empty-week message now names the week, and a missing or disabled event source is reported in its delivery status instead of an empty email being sent. With an older Fair Events, Fair Audience keeps rendering the email itself.
