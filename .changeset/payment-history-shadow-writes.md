Record payment history as payments happen. Every new payment attempt is now also linked to the purchase it pays for, and participants signed up through Fair Audience alone (its own purchase route, or added by an organizer) get a matching ticket purchase that follows them. Ticket counts, capacity, Statistics, payments and emails stay the same. Update Fair Events and Fair Audience together: recording starts once both are on this release.
