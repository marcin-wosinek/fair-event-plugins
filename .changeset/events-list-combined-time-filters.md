Events List block: the time filter is now three checkboxes — Past, Current and Future — so a list can show, for example, events happening now together with upcoming ones. Events matching any checked period are listed once. Clearing every checkbox shows no events, and the editor asks to check at least one period. Existing blocks keep their selection and output: "All events" becomes all three periods, and Upcoming, Ongoing and Past become Future, Current and Past.
