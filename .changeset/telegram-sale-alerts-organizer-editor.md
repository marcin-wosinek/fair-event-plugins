Telegram ticket sale alerts are now set up with each organizer: on Fair Events → Organizers when organizer management is on, otherwise under Settings → Organizer for the sitewide organizer. "Send test message", below the chat or channel field, posts a labelled sample to the chat or channel in the field, even before it is saved and while alerts are off, and shows the result next to the field. Settings, schedules and sales are not changed. The sitewide "Send ticket sale alerts to Telegram" switch is removed: each organizer's "Send this organizer's sales" switch now turns both its immediate messages and its digests on or off. Sites upgrading with the sitewide switch off have every organizer switched off, keeping its chat and schedule. Sites with the switch on keep each organizer's switch. Fair Events → Event notifications still lists recent ticket sale alerts and links to where organizers are set up.
