Event Notifications, Event Feeds, Financial Entries and Payment Transactions now share one list layout: the page title with its add and import actions beside it, then a toolbar with search and the frequent filters, More filters for the less-used ones, Clear filters while a filter is active, and the result count ("51–100 of 236 transactions"). A summary lists the filters behind the shown rows, including those inside a collapsed More filters, and pagination sits below the table on the right. Selected transactions show their count next to Export Selected. Empty lists offer their first step (add or import), and lists with no matches offer to clear the filters. Each page keeps its existing search, filters, sorting, pagination and actions.
