Weekly Telegram notifications are easier to read: the calendar title is a link, the date range has its own line, and each event is a bullet with its weekday, time and linked title instead of `*` markers and full URLs. Titles with characters such as `*`, `_` or `[` are shown exactly as written. The "Send test summary to Telegram" button now sends the next scheduled week's real summary, and the preview shows each Telegram message with its links. The Events Week "copy summary" text is unchanged.
