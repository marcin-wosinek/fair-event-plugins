Add per-feed WhatsApp sharing reminders. With the WhatsApp feature of Fair Events Experimental on, Event notifications links to WhatsApp sharing reminders, where an administrator designates one collaborator per event feed (a team member or a name and number), confirms their consent, chooses the approved template whose button opens the feed's weekly summary, and sets the weekly day and time. Each scheduled week is sent at most once, even after a timeout or a replay; skipped and failed runs say why, and the history shows WhatsApp's acceptance and delivery updates. A renamed feed or a changed number pauses its reminder until it is confirmed again.
