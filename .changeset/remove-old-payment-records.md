Fair Events → Payment history can now remove the records only the payment history migration used: Fair Audience's old transaction ledger, the migration's verification report and its state, and the findings of dry runs. An administrator starts it from the Upgrade readiness card once the site is safe to upgrade, nothing is left to review and the staging checks are recorded, after confirming a fresh backup. Findings about Fair Audience payment records keep a copy of the record. Purchases, payment links, findings to review, registrations and fee payments stay as they are, and running it again changes nothing. New installations no longer create the verification report table.
