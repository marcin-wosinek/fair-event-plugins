Add a budget selector to the Manage Event Finance tab, applying to the whole event/series. When Fair Finance pre-generates a reconciliation split for a settlement transfer, or matches a bank entry to a single transaction, each allocation now preselects the budget linked to its payment's event (falling back to no budget when the event has none, is unlinked, or its budget was deleted). Organisers can still override any preselected budget before saving.
