Event notifications now open on a searchable list of every notification, showing where each one sends, whether it is on or needs attention, its next send and its latest scheduled send or test. Each notification opens on its own page with Settings and History tabs, and the history of all notifications can be filtered by notification, kind, outcome, scheduled or test, and date, with the details of each send down to every recipient or chat. Unsaved changes are kept while moving between these views, and every view has its own link.
