Administrators can manage several organizers. With Fair Events Experimental active, the Organizer tab of Fair Events Settings becomes an Organizers list: the sitewide organizer stays the default, and additional organizers can be added and edited, each with its own name, identity details and private notification email addresses. Additional organizers start blank and copy nothing from the default. The new "Organizers" bundle on the Experimental tab is on by default; turning it off brings back the single Organizer tab and keeps every organizer for when it is turned on again. Fair Events on its own still shows one sitewide organizer, with its form unchanged.
