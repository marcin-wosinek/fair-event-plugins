Administrators can manage several organizers. With Fair Events Experimental active and its new "Organizers" bundle turned on in the Experimental settings tab, an Organizers page appears in the Fair Events menu just above Settings and replaces the Organizer settings tab; links to the old tab open the new page. The sitewide organizer stays the default, and additional organizers can be added and edited, each with its own name, identity details and private notification email addresses. Additional organizers start blank and copy nothing from the default. The bundle is off by default, so activating the plugin changes nothing on its own; a saved choice is kept. Turning it off brings back the single Organizer tab and keeps every organizer for when it is turned on again. Fair Events on its own still shows one sitewide organizer, with its form unchanged.
