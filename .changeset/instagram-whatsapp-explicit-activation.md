'fair-events-experimental': minor
---

Instagram and WhatsApp now stay off until an administrator turns them on in Fair Events → Settings → Experimental. A connection already stored in Fair Audience no longer turns Instagram on by itself; Fair Audience keeps managing it until then. WhatsApp gets its own default-off toggle: while it is off, Fair Events → WhatsApp, its Connectors entry, its REST routes, its webhook and its daily cleanup are not registered, and the saved IDs, credentials and delivery history are kept. Choices already saved stay as they are. Fair Events → Instagram now says why it cannot manage the connection. It recommends updating Fair Audience only when the installed Fair Audience lacks the handoff, and gives separate steps when an update has not taken effect yet or the site's response was incomplete.
