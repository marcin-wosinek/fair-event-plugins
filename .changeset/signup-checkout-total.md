Show one checkout total directly above the Event Signup submit button. It is always visible — including a zero amount for free signups — and updates as soon as the visitor changes the ticket type, quantity, dates, or activities, with member discounts included once they load. It replaces the separate totals that used to appear inside the date and activity sections. Site-level conversion tracking, such as a Meta event, can read the payable amount from `.fair-events-signup-checkout-total` through its `data-amount` and `data-currency` attributes; the plugin itself sends no tracking data.
