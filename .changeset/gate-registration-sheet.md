The List tab of Manage Event can print a gate sheet: the ticket and extra-option prices that apply when the event starts, in the event's currency, followed by two blank pages for writing down people who register and pay at the gate. Missing prices are marked as not configured rather than free, unavailable ticket types are marked as such, and when the event start is outside a single pricing period the organizer chooses one. It works before anyone signs up and changes no data.
