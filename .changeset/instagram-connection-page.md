Manage the Instagram connection in Fair Events → Instagram. Connecting, the account details, testing and disconnecting move there from Fair Audience → Settings → Instagram, which now links to the new page. An existing connection keeps working without re-entering it, and manual posts in Fair Audience → Instagram Posts keep publishing with the same account. Turn the page on with the new Instagram setting in Fair Events → Settings → Experimental. It is off by default, including on sites that are already connected, where Fair Audience keeps managing the connection until it is turned on.
