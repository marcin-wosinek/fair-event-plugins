Hide the Event Signup checkout total while the "Get Tickets" button is disabled. The total now appears together with a usable button — once the visitor's pricing has loaded and the required activities or dates are chosen — and disappears again if a later choice makes the form incomplete or while a purchase is being processed. A free signup still shows an explicit zero. The machine-readable amount and currency on the total stay current while it is hidden, so site-level tracking is unaffected.
