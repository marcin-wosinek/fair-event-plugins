The Mailing Signup block can offer the weekly email of upcoming events. In the block's new "Weekly update" settings, editors show a "Receive a weekly update of upcoming events" checkbox, unchecked or checked by default, and either let subscribers choose event feeds or subscribe them to one feed without showing a choice. A checked weekly update needs at least one feed; the choice applies when the subscriber confirms their email address, and an already-subscribed address is emailed a link to change its preferences instead. When the form's feeds are not available, it says so and signs up without the weekly update. Existing blocks keep working unchanged. The form now also shows the right message when only a management link is sent or a signed-in subscriber's preferences are updated.
