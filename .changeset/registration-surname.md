Event registration asks for an optional surname next to the name and stores the two separately. Organizers can edit both from the List tab's new "Edit name" action, and lists, exports, emails and payment history show the complete name. Existing registrations keep their names exactly as entered, with no surname. Fair Audience creates new participants with a separate surname and pre-fills the two fields separately.
