**Event Signup** now explains what each series ticket covers before purchase. Every ticket of a recurring event says whether it is for one date, selected dates or the whole series, and whether its price is per date or for the whole series. The matching date control follows the ticket choices directly: a date dropdown, date checkboxes with their minimum explained, or the list of dates a whole-series ticket includes. A summary above the total names the selected ticket and the dates it covers, and updates when the ticket or dates change.

Full dates stay listed, marked full, and cannot be chosen. When a whole-series ticket cannot be bought because one of its dates is full, the summary names that date instead of leaving the button disabled without a reason. Availability is refreshed after the page loads and after a refused checkout, so a cached page never offers a date that has since filled up. Checkout keeps applying the same prices, sale periods and capacity rules as before.
