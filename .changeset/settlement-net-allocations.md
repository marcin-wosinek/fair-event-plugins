Keep net payment allocations when a Mollie settlement includes adjustments such as invoice compensation. When the fees recorded on each payment match the payment fees in the settlement evidence, every payment is allocated at its net amount, and each separately listed adjustment gets its own allocation. Fees are not deducted twice. Missing or disagreeing fees, and unexplained differences, are explained instead of balanced. Settlements that are already split keep their existing allocations.
