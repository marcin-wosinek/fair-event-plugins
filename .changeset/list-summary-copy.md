Add a Summary button to the event List that copies ticket totals as plain text, ready to paste into a message: the number of confirmed tickets, the tickets sold per ticket type and the tickets holding each add-on, in their configured order and including zero counts. Every confirmed ticket counts, whatever the mailing filter shows, and only names and counts are copied, never participant details. Confirmed test-mode purchases are left out of these totals and counted in a separate "Test payments" section. The button explains when copying fails or when the summary could be wrong, for example while older registrations are still being split into tickets. On a date of a series, the List now shows the series' add-on columns.
