Each subject in Custom Mail's Mail History now links to a read-only view of that sent email: its saved subject and formatted content, who it was sent to, when, and how many emails were sent, failed or skipped. The view has its own address, so it can be opened in another tab or bookmarked, and returning to Mail History keeps the email being written. Placeholders are shown as written, since they were filled in separately for each recipient.
