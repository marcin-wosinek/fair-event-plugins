Count activity places per ticket. Several tickets bought together each take their own place in the activities they select, and the signup form asks for each ticket's activities, with a button that applies the first ticket's choice to all. Every purchase, added activity, retried payment, move and admin edit checks the activity's remaining places, so buying at the same moment can no longer overbook it. On recurring events each date has its own activity places, and a whole-series ticket takes one on every date it covers. An administrator can go past an activity's limit only by giving a reason, and the change is shown as over capacity. A payment that arrives after its place was taken is still honored and flagged. A failed payment for added activities releases their places at once.
