Prepare Fair Audience signups, added activities and series pass upgrades for Fair Events owning their payment history. Once the payment history cutover is activated (in a later release), each of these payments is recorded before the buyer pays, Fair Events confirms the purchase, its activities or its upgrade once however often the payment notification arrives, and the registration follows. A series pass upgrade then costs the pass price minus what that purchase itself paid, and a retried payment for several dates covers all of them. Nothing changes until the cutover is activated.
