Reorder add-ons from a popup in Manage Event → Prices. The Add-ons section now has a **Reorder add-ons** button that opens a compact list of the add-ons: drag one by its handle (mouse or touch) or use its up and down arrow buttons to place it anywhere. The Prices table follows each move straight away, and the new order is saved together with the rest of the tab by **Save tickets** — closing the popup neither saves nor discards anything. The signup form lists add-ons in the saved order, and prices, capacity, collaborators and existing signups stay with their add-on. **Move to top** remains as a shortcut.
