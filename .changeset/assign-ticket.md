Administrators can now give one ticket of a purchase to someone else from the Audience tab. "Assign ticket" on a ticket shows who bought it and who holds it, and lets you pick any existing participant — including the purchaser, to take it back — or add a new one in the same step. If the email you enter already belongs to someone, you are offered that person instead of a duplicate. Only the chosen ticket changes hands: the purchase, its payment and its other tickets stay with the purchaser, who keeps receiving confirmation and refund messages. The ticket then appears under its new holder with "Purchased by …", and a purchaser who has given away every ticket is listed as the purchaser rather than as attending. A checked-in ticket has to have its check-in cleared first, and tickets that failed, expired, were cancelled or refunded cannot be assigned.
