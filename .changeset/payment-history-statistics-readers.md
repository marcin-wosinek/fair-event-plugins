'fair-events': minor

Event statistics can now read Fair Events' own records and work without Fair Audience. After payment records are switched to Fair Events, an administrator can switch statistics too on the Payment history page; a site that never had Fair Audience records uses Fair Events' records from the start. Statistics then count only Fair Events tickets and activities, and revenue counts each date's own share of a payment. Revenue now says when it is unavailable because payment information can't be read, instead of showing 0, and when some paid payments aren't linked to the event's purchases yet, with a link to review them. Whole-series pass revenue now counts once, on the series' first date, and other dates of the series show it separately, so their revenue totals drop by the pass amount.
