Show each ticket as its own row in the event Audience tab, under the purchaser. Every ticket row shows its reference, type, activities, and status. It has its own check-in box and an "Edit ticket" action, while participant actions stay on the participant row as "Edit participant", "Delete", and "Move". Activity totals now count tickets. Activities not tied to a ticket are totalled separately and appear on a "Not tied to a ticket" row. The printed list gives each ticket its own row. On narrow screens the table stacks into labelled cards.
