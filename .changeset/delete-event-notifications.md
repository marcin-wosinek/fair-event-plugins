Event notifications can now be deleted from the notification list. A confirmation names the notification and explains that its queued sends are cancelled, that a send already started finishes, that delivered messages cannot be recalled, and that its history stays in All notification history. Deletion removes only that notification and works while its delivery plugin is inactive; a save or a scheduled send running at the same time can no longer bring a deleted notification back or leave a send queued for it.
