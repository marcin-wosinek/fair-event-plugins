Organizers can now be an individual person, a company or an NGO, alongside an organization or a sports club. The Organization type field is now called Organizer type, and the choice applies to the sitewide organizer and to additional organizers alike. Saved organizers keep their type, and new ones still start as an organization. A person's structured data uses the Schema.org Person type, with the logo published as the person's image.
