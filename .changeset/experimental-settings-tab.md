Fair Events Experimental settings now live in an Experimental tab of Fair Events → Settings instead of their own menu entry. The tab holds the same feature toggles, weekly notifications and Meta Conversions settings, with their saved values unchanged, and only administrators can open it. Links to the former Experimental Settings page redirect to the new tab, and the selected tab is kept on reload and in bookmarks. Without Fair Events Experimental, the Settings page shows its other tabs as before. The Settings tabs now wrap onto a second row on narrow screens.
