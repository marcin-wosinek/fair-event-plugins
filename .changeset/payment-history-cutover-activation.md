Add the switch of payment records to Fair Events on the Payment history page. Once the migration is verified and a fresh database backup is confirmed, an administrator can make Fair Events decide which purchases are paid, for tickets and for Fair Audience registrations, activities and series upgrades. The switch refuses while anything blocks it, including a Fair Audience too old to record its payments in Fair Events, is recorded in the audit, and can't be undone; the page then shows who switched, when, and anything that needs fixing.
