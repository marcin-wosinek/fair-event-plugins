Team member profiles (Fair Events → Team members) gain an optional website, a plain-text bio that keeps its line breaks, and a portrait chosen from the Media Library with preview, replace and remove actions. Removing a portrait keeps the image in the Media Library. Instagram handles are now checked when they are added or changed; existing handles stay editable. A new reader returns only the public promotional part of profiles (display name, Instagram handle, website, bio and portrait) for Event Content Studio campaign facts. Contact details and grammatical gender stay private. Existing profiles and event teams are kept when the site upgrades.
