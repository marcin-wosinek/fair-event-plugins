Team members now take roles and an order. In Manage Event → Event Details, organizers add a person with a role such as `teacher` or `organizer`, may give the same person several roles, and reorder the list with Move up and Move down; repeating a person's role is refused. Saved schedule items get their own ordered contributors in the Schedule tab. A series keeps both on the series: its dates show them read-only and link to where they are managed. Existing team members keep their place without a role, and profiles referenced by an event or a programme item cannot be deleted. Copies take the contributors along, removed schedule items lose theirs, and Fair Events now passes companion plugins the copied schedule items and reports removed ones.
