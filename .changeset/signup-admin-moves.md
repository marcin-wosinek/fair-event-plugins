Administrators can now move a signup to another date of its series, or give it another ticket type, from the Manage Event List tab. The signup and all its tickets except cancelled or refunded ones change together, and each choice shows how many places are left. When the chosen date or ticket type is full, the change needs a reason: it is then saved, the signup is marked "Confirmed — over capacity", and a details link shows who went over capacity, when, and why. A signup flagged because its payment arrived after its hold expired says so. The amount paid never changes. Fair Audience moves or updates the participant's registration to match, so the Audience tab agrees with the List.
