Manage single tickets from the Audience tab. Each ticket row now offers **Move ticket** (to another date of the same recurring event, with a required reason when the target date or an activity is full), **Cancel ticket** and, once cancelled, **Delete ticket**. Every action changes only the ticket it names: the purchase, its payment and its other tickets stay as they are, nothing is refunded automatically, and the popups say so before anything changes. A participant left without tickets stays in the audience, no longer listed as signed up.

The Audience search now finds participants and tickets by purchaser, assignee, email and ticket reference and shows only the matching tickets, and **Export tickets CSV** downloads those tickets one per row with their purchaser and assignee.
