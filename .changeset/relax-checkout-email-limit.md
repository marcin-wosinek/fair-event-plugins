Buyers are no longer locked out of ticket checkout after a few attempts. One email can now start 10 checkouts — up from 3 — and can check out again 15 minutes after the last one instead of an hour. Only a checkout that actually saved a signup counts: an attempt rejected because a ticket sold out, a selection was incomplete or the form had an error no longer uses up the allowance, so a buyer can correct the form and complete the purchase with the same email. Repeating a checkout that already exists still counts nothing. The separate limit of 20 attempts per hour from one IP address is unchanged.
