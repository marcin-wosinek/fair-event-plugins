Fair Events no longer scans or re-synchronizes old purchase history left over from the payment history migration. Participants that organizers add, relabel, move, retype or remove in Fair Audience now update the matching Fair Events purchase (capacity and Statistics) immediately, instead of up to fifteen minutes later. Payments, confirmations and organizer changes that a request couldn't finish are still retried in the background, without creating duplicate purchases or emails. Update Fair Events and Fair Audience together.
