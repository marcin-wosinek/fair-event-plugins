Adding questionnaire respondents to a group now lists everyone who will be added, says up front which responses are left out and that existing members are skipped, and reports who was added, who was skipped and who could not be added with the reason. Only the participants that failed stay in the popup for another try, against the same group, so retrying after creating a new group no longer creates a second one. The popup can't be closed while it's working, and the result appears next to the button once everything has gone through.
