Purchase payments now read Fair Events' own records once statistics use them. Manage Event's Finance tab keeps its payments list and adds a Purchase payments section with every payment attempt of each purchase, its share of a shared payment and whether it counts as revenue. A payment's transaction page shows which purchases it pays for. A participant's page lists what they bought, with every payment, instead of only each registration's latest attempt. A buyer returning from an earlier payment attempt sees that attempt's purchase, and payment notifications name the event, ticket type and activities from Fair Events' records.
