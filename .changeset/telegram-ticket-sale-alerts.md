Fair Events → Event notifications can now post a Telegram message to an event's organizer for each paid ticket purchase. The message names the event, its dates, each ticket type with its quantity and the total paid, never the buyer. Each organizer has its own switch and private chat or channel; events without an organizer of their own go to the sitewide organizer, and a sale is never sent to another organizer's chat. Ticket sale alerts are off until an administrator turns them on, a replayed payment never sends a sale twice, and recent alerts show whether each was sent, skipped and why, failed or has an unknown outcome. Checkout never waits for Telegram.
