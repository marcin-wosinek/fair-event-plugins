The event page now follows a transferred ticket's holder. A purchaser who gave all their tickets for a date to someone else is no longer shown as signed up, with no old ticket type and no form to add activities, and can buy a new ticket. They can no longer add activities to tickets they gave away. The new holder is shown as signed up, with the ticket and its activities, and can add free or paid activities to it. A paid activity added by the holder is recorded as an additional charge on the original purchase. A whole-series pass that was given to someone else covers its new holder's dates and no longer its buyer's. A page left open from before a transfer, or a retried payment for activities, adds nothing to a ticket the visitor no longer holds. A payment that was already started is still applied to the ticket it was made for. Purchases, payments, transfer history and sibling tickets stay as they were.
