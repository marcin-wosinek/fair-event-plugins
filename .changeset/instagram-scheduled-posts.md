Event notifications can now publish the week's events to Instagram. Add an Instagram notification, choose the event feed, heading page and schedule, and preview the image and caption without publishing. Once turned on, each week is posted once as one image with the list in its caption. Weeks without events are skipped, and failures (no connection, too many events to fit, Instagram errors) appear in the notification's history. Requires the Instagram feature of Fair Events Experimental.
