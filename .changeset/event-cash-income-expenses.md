The event's Finance tab has a new "Income and expenses" section for administrators. Record cash received at the event, such as a day's gate sales, and expenses paid from event cash or from an organizer's personal funds, with the payer's name. The section shows cash income, cash expenses, net cash movement, total expenses and personally paid amounts by payer, and the event's Total Income now adds recorded cash income to online payments. The operations are ordinary Fair Finance accounting entries, work without Fair Payments Connector, and existing entries keep their meaning. Fair Finance's entry list and editor show and keep each entry's payment source, and entries recorded as event cash or personally paid cannot be split, matched to online payments or reconciled with a settlement.
